What I do

Career bio

I'm a data scientist who lives at the crossroads of numbers, human behavior, and — always — games. I believe the best insights don't just explain the past; they shape experiences people actually love.

Today I work on the Engagement team at Discord as a data scientist, using analysis and modeling to understand how communities grow, what keeps players coming back, and where the magic happens. Discord is the ultimate gaming hub, and turning gameplay data into real impact is my kind of work.

Before Discord, I worked across the entertainment and tech industry in LA and the Bay Area, building a career around data-driven storytelling — always drawn to spaces where culture, creativity, and analytics intersect. And yes, games have always been the thread running through it all.

I studied at the USC Marshall School of Business, where I led research at the Annenberg Innovation Lab. It's where I first learned that great data work isn't about dashboards — it's about asking the right questions.
